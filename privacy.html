Privacy policy

NeuroSpine Calculator · Dr. Mohammed Asfa · Last updated 26 November 2026

Summary

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal or health information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Everything you type into a calculator, drug-dose page or localizer is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere. The app saves a few settings in your phone's local storage so they are kept between visits:

  • your favourite and recently opened tools, and which sections are open;
  • the specialty filter, the child or adult setting on drug doses, and the 3D view settings;
  • that you have read the first-use notice.

It also keeps a copy of its own files (pages, pictures and 3D models) so it works offline. You can remove all of this by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Network requests

  • The app's files are downloaded from GitHub Pages (hasfa91-coder.github.io). Like any website host, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address; see the GitHub privacy statement.
  • The text font is downloaded from Google Fonts, which may likewise record your IP address.
  • Links to references (for example PubMed or a journal) open the outside website only when you tap them.

Children

The app is a reference tool for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.

Medical disclaimer

The app is for education and clinical reference by healthcare professionals. Scores, doses and classifications support clinical judgement and do not replace it. Always check results against the original sources, the product label and your local protocols. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any patient.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
